Adaptation: retain only noun forms and adjective forms occurring in this training corpus; preserve lemma, gender, number, and homograph part-of-speech flags. Context filtering and gender selection are rules, not a full syntactic parser. Uncertain homographs are omitted; dual-gender forms are marked ambiguous when context cannot decide. Content answers and grading rules are unchanged.

Source TSV SHA-256: 637ba37a767a66679c48371d673ece50cbf541b49a4e40e598963d4f3fbce52b

Supplemental reviewed common-noun forms and sense distinctions are included because Lexique leaves some homograph genders blank. Examples: aide (assistance vs assistant), mémoire (memory vs report), livre (book vs pound), poste (job vs postal service), gens (special agreement), Internet.
